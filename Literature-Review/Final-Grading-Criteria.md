# Final Grading Criteria

The project grade is based on the following standard evaluation components:

## 1. Documentation (40%)
- Project Planning & Management documents
- Literature Review summary and academic references
- Requirements analysis
- System design diagrams
- Structured technical reporting

## 2. Implementation & Technical Work (35%)
- Quality of penetration testing methodology
- Nmap scanning, enumeration, exploitation attempts
- Evidence collection (screenshots, logs, outputs)
- Correctness and reproducibility of steps

## 3. Testing & Validation (15%)
- Test cases and expected results
- Validation of findings
- Explanation of impact and severity of discovered vulnerabilities

## 4. Presentation & Delivery (10%)
- Clarity of final project presentation
- Structure, visuals, and delivery
- Confidence in explaining methodology and results

---

### ✔ Total: 100%
