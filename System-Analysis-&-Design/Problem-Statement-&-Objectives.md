# Problem Statement & Objectives

## Problem Statement
Many organizations fail to identify vulnerabilities in their networks due to lack of controlled testing environments. This project simulates real-world penetration testing in a safe, isolated lab using Metasploitable 2 as the target. The goal is to identify vulnerabilities, exploit them safely, and provide actionable recommendations.

## Objectives
- Conduct comprehensive port scanning and service enumeration.  
- Identify and document exploitable vulnerabilities.  
- Safely exploit selected vulnerabilities to demonstrate security impact.  
- Collect and organize evidence (logs, screenshots, outputs).  
- Produce a detailed, professional report including remediation recommendations.
